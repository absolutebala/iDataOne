MeraKanakku Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

MeraKanakku is a money-tracking app for small shop owners, published by iDataOne. This policy explains what the app reads, why, and where it is kept. In short: everything stays on your phone. We have no servers, no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we never receive your data.

What the app reads and why

SMS (only if you allow it): MeraKanakku reads bank and UPI messages to record money your shop receives and pays — the amount, the sender or receiver name / UPI ID, the reference number and the date. Personal messages, OTPs and promotions are ignored and not stored.

Payment notifications (only if you turn on notification access): MeraKanakku reads notifications from payment apps (such as Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), WhatsApp payments and bank apps, to record UPI payments that arrive without an SMS. Notifications from all other apps are ignored. The text of recent payment notifications is kept on the phone so you can check how each entry was recorded.

Contacts (only if you allow it): used only to fill in a customer’s name and phone number when you add or edit a customer. Only the contact you pick is saved in MeraKanakku.

What you enter: your shop name, your UPI ID, customers (name, Mr/Mrs, phone number), vendors, sales, bills, dues, payments and expenses.

Where your data is kept

All of this is stored only in the app’s private storage on your phone. It is not uploaded to us or to anyone else. MeraKanakku does not have a server or user accounts and contains no advertising or analytics tools.

If you have turned on Android’s backup to your Google account, Android may include the app’s data in that backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup belongs to you and is handled by Google under its own policy; we cannot see it.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data. When you tap Remind, Save & Share or Save & Send, MeraKanakku opens WhatsApp (or SMS) with a message that you then send yourself to your customer. Those apps handle that message under their own privacy policies.

Your choices

SMS, contacts and notification access are optional. You can refuse or turn them off at any time in Android Settings → Apps → MeraKanakku; the app keeps working and you can enter payments by hand.

To delete all MeraKanakku data, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → MeraKanakku → Storage. Because we hold no copy, deleting it on your phone deletes it completely (apart from any Android backup you control).

Security

Your data is protected by your phone’s own security. We recommend keeping a screen lock on the phone you use for your shop.

Children

MeraKanakku is made for shop owners and is not directed at children under 18.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be shown in the app and on this page with a new date.

பண அறிவிப்புகள் (அறிவிப்பு அணுகலை இயக்கினால் மட்டும்): SMS வராத UPI பணத்தைப் பதிவு செய்ய, பண ஆப்கள் (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM போன்றவை), WhatsApp பணம், வங்கி ஆப்களின் அறிவிப்புகளைப் படிக்கிறது. மற்ற ஆப் அறிவிப்புகள் புறக்கணிக்கப்படும். ஒவ்வொரு பதிவும் எப்படி வந்தது என்று சரிபார்க்க, சமீபத்திய பண அறிவிப்புகளின் உரை மொபைலில் வைக்கப்படும்.

தொடர்புகள் (நீங்கள் அனுமதித்தால் மட்டும்): வாடிக்கையாளரைச் சேர்க்கும்போது பெயர், மொபைல் எண்ணை நிரப்ப மட்டும். நீங்கள் தேர்வு செய்யும் தொடர்பு மட்டுமே சேமிக்கப்படும்.

நீங்கள் உள்ளிடுவது: கடை பெயர், UPI ID, வாடிக்கையாளர்கள் (பெயர், திரு/திருமதி, மொபைல் எண்), சப்ளையர்கள், விற்பனை, பில்கள், நிலுவைகள், பணம், செலவுகள்.
